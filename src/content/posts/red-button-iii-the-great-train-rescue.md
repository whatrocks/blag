## This year's new film-making technique

Year one was reversing a clip ([Red Button](/red-button)) to create the time-travel effect, and year two was learning how to use a green screen ([Red Button II](/red-button-ii-the-giant-of-belvedere)). This year was two layers of greenscreens. I know, I know, a little boring, but it did allow me to create this amazing scene:

![Penny in Giant's pocket](/img/rb3/pocket.png)

## The festival

The [Belvedere Home-Made Film Festival](https://bhmff.com) was delightful, as usual. The heat wave in the Bay broke that day, which made the hot cocoa and home-made soup yummier. I had some happy campers with me.

![Festival](/img/rb3/show.jpeg)

Making movies is fun! I love my family!
